---
name: Feed layout priorities
description: Approved layout direction for the news feed and its filtering controls.
---

Prioritize news immediately below a compact “Notícias Verificadas” header, while preserving the existing overall content width. Keep filters available but collapsed by default, next to the administrator's manual update control.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested and approved reducing the permanent space occupied by filters so articles appear earlier without widening or narrowing the overall feed.

**How to apply:** Keep filters accessible to every reader, retain administrator-only collection, and accommodate phone layouts and enlarged reading text without horizontal overflow. Category and subcategory selection should remain easy without a permanently expanded filter section.

Use a restrained default text scale and compact card spacing so more articles fit on the initial screen, while retaining comfortable mobile reading and optional enlarged text.

**Why:** The user found the default lettering too large and explicitly requested a denser, harmonious feed that remains easy to read on phones.

**How to apply:** Preserve hierarchy and legibility instead of uniformly shrinking every label. Keep enlarged reading preferences functional, and preserve the overall feed width when changing the root text scale.
